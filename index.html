Featured Project

CRM Data Governance Case Study

The primary project now shows operational judgment: auditing CRM completeness, separating stale-record assumptions from governance problems, and turning findings into a 30-60-90 remediation plan.

Business Problem

CRM records had missing contact fields, inconsistent property usage, placeholder values, and reporting risks that could affect follow-up reliability.

Investigation

Audited 883 records, segmented missing fields by severity, and prioritized records based on contactability and operational impact.

Recommendation

Prevent bad data at entry, standardize field use, and consolidate duplicate properties through a phased governance plan.
