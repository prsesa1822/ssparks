Workflow

Evidence before embellishment.

The user supplies a source résumé and target job posting. The product analyzes role alignment, identifies strengths and honest gaps, produces a tailored résumé and cover letter, and runs an integrity review before the user decides what to keep.

Fit & gap scan

Compares source evidence with the role and separates supported strengths from gaps.

Tailored drafts

Builds a targeted résumé and cover letter from the provided materials.

Integrity review

Flags output that appears unsupported, vague, or weakened so the user can review it.
