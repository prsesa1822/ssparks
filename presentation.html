VISUALIZATION 1 - TREND
LINE
Cremation rates rose steadily from 57.5% in 2021 to
63.4% in 2025, reflecting a consistent national shift
away from traditional burial practices.
Burial rates declined significantly over the same
period, falling from 42.2% in 2021 to just 31.6% in
2025. The trend lines show a clear and widening
divergence between the two methods.
This visualization confirms that cremation is not just
a temporary trend - it represents a structural, long-
term shift in end-of-life preferences across the
United States.
Page 5
PREFERENCE GAP
The gap between cremation and burial preference has
widened significantly from 2021 to 2025, highlighting
a clear and accelerating shift in end-of-life choices.
- 2021: 15.3 percentage point gap
- 2022: 18.0 percentage point gap
- 2023: 21.0 percentage point gap
- 2024: 28.6 percentage point gap
- 2025: 31.8 percentage point gap
Over just five years, the preference gap more than
doubled - from 15.3% to 31.8% - underscoring a
decisive and sustained trend toward cremation across
the United States.
Page 6
POTENTIAL DRIVERS OF
CREMATION GROWTH
Possible contributing factors include:
- Lower average cost compared to traditional burial
- Geographic mobility of families
- Reduced religious restrictions
- Simpler memorialization options
- Changing consumer preferences
Further research would be required to quantify the impact of each factor.
Page 7
Each year's data was sourced from industry reports
including CANA and NFDA. Data types vary by year:
2021-2022 are marked as actual, while 2023-2025
include estimates and projections. Confidence ratings
range from Medium to High across the dataset.
COVID-era data (2021-2022) shows notable
variation due to pandemic-related mortality shifts.
Preferred sources are CANA (Cremation
Association of North America) and NFDA (National
Funeral Directors Association) for highest
reliability. Always cross-reference estimates with
actual figures when available.

AI PROJECT
SUMMARY
AI made learning visible at every stage of this project - from
defining the research question to building visualizations and
crafting the final portfolio narrative.
Six key areas where AI provided coaching support:
- Planning: Defining scope and research question
- Data Organization: Google Sheets structure and formulas
- Source Awareness: Tracking and validating data sources
- Tableau Skills: Building connections and visualizations
- Troubleshooting: Diagnosing and resolving stuck points
- Storytelling: Crafting a professional portfolio narrative
Big Takeaway: AI coached the process but did not replace
the learning. Every insight, decision, and skill demonstrated in
this portfolio reflects genuine analyst development - AI
simply made that growth faster and more visible.
