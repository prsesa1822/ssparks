Interpretation

What the comparison supports—and what it does not.

Supported

The two projected rates differ by 31.8 percentage points, calculated as 63.4 minus 31.6.

Useful context

The figures provide a concise industry-level comparison that can help frame changing customer preferences.

Limitations

The comparison does not explain regional variation, customer motivation, or final 2025 outcomes. No causal claim is made.
