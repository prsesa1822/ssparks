Project Summary

Turning a chaotic job search into a trackable operating system.

Dragon's Hoard Tracker started as a simple job application tracker because managing applications through screenshots, scattered notes, and memory was not sustainable.

It evolved into a career operations platform with CRM-style workflows, analytics dashboards, recruiter tracking, knowledge management, achievement systems, and hidden easter eggs.

Core Features

CRM workflows, analytics, and career knowledge in one place.

Quest Log

CRM-style job application pipeline with custom stages, opportunity status tracking, and structured follow-up habits.

Dragon Score

Opportunity scoring framework for evaluating role fit across remote compatibility, customer success alignment, CRM relevance, AI exposure, salary potential, and growth opportunity.

Quest Details

Individual opportunity pages for notes, timelines, recruiter context, lessons learned, and next-step planning.

Recruiter Intel

Contact tracking and relationship notes that keep recruiter conversations visible, searchable, and follow-up ready.

Ability Scores

Analytics dashboard for application volume, response rates, status mix, score distribution, and career progress metrics.

Hall of Glory

Achievement system that rewards persistence, learning, and career milestones while making repetitive work easier to return to.

Knowledge Management

The Grimoire turns lessons into reusable assets.

Spellbook Templates

Reusable structures for outreach, follow-up, interview preparation, and job-search workflows.

Bestiary

Company, recruiter, ghost, dragon, and suspicious mimic tracking for pattern recognition and relationship context.

Battle Reports

Interview retrospectives that capture what happened, what worked, what changed, and what to improve next time.

Prophecies

Long-term career goals focused on Customer Success, Operations, CRM, Data, and AI-enabled work.

Lore Archives

Project history, lessons, milestones, and decision context preserved for future reflection and portfolio storytelling.

Legendary Artifacts

Notable project assets, career materials, and hidden lore elements that reward curiosity without distracting from business value.

Why It Matters

A creative UX layer over real business operations concepts.

Dragon's Hoard Tracker demonstrates how a stressful, unstructured process can become a managed operating system. The fantasy theme makes the experience more memorable, but the underlying work is practical: define the pipeline, score opportunities, track relationships, measure progress, document lessons, and use the data to make better decisions.

For hiring managers, the project shows CRM judgment, customer operations instincts, product ownership, and comfort using AI-assisted tools to move from idea to working application.
