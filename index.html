Featured Project

Local AI Infrastructure & Personal Automation

A self-hosted AI workspace runs Open WebUI, Ollama, local LLMs, and RAG knowledge collections on local hardware, alongside a separate Tasker-to-Telegram reminder automation for real-world clock-in and clock-out prompts. It demonstrates systems thinking across mobile automation, API integration, containerized services, and local AI operations.

What it does

A Tasker workplace geofence sends clock-in and clock-out reminders directly through a Telegram bot, turning location events into practical prompts without relying on memory alone.

Why it matters

The AI layer runs locally through Open WebUI and Ollama with Llama 3.1 8B, Gemma 3 12B, Phi-4, Qwen3, and two RAG knowledge collections - no cloud dependency for inference.
