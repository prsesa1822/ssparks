Case studyCRM data quality & customer operations

Finding contactability risk inside everyday CRM work.

I manage active accounts and maintain customer records in a regulated, high-emotion environment. A self-initiated HubSpot Breeze AI audit helped me quantify missing data and structure a practical remediation proposal.

Scope: the audit and 30-60-90 plan were self-directed. The plan was proposed and was not formally adopted, so this case study does not claim company-wide governance or measured downstream impact.

Self-initiated audit

What the records showed.

Completeness gap

Across 883 reviewed records, 811 were missing email addresses. Additional missing or inconsistent fields included phone, address, job title, birthday, placeholder values, duplicate properties, and unclear sources of truth.

Open redacted audit exhibit

Root-cause view

The redacted export suggested a current completeness and consistency problem rather than a simple backlog of old records. That distinction shaped the proposed plan.

Open redacted root-cause exhibit

Proposed remediation

A 30-60-90 plan structured for leadership review.

First 30 days

Define and prioritize

Confirm required fields, sources of truth, contactability definitions, ownership, and cleanup priorities.

Days 31–60

Clean and standardize

Work through high-value records, normalize conventions, remove placeholders, and document exceptions.

Days 61–90

Prevent recurrence

Propose validation rules, lightweight audits, ownership expectations, and simple quality checks.
