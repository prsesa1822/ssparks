Death Care in Data

A portfolio case study translating national cremation and burial preference data into a clear business story. The project combines spreadsheet organization, source review, Tableau visualization, and professional context from the death care field.

Project Overview

U.S. Cremation vs. Burial Trends, 2021-2025

The central question was straightforward: how have cremation and burial rates changed over the last five years, and what does that suggest about changing end-of-life preferences?

The topic connects national trend data with Sarah's firsthand work supporting families as they make end-of-life decisions.

Key Findings

57.5% to 63.4%Cremation increased from 2021 to 2025.
42.2% to 31.6%Burial declined over the same period.
31.8 ptsCremation's preference gap over burial by 2025.

Workflow

1. Organize

Built a focused dataset in Google Sheets with year, cremation rate, burial rate, source, data type, and confidence fields.

2. Visualize

Used Tableau Public to create trend, preference gap, and source confidence views for clear storytelling.

3. Explain

Translated the visuals into a concise portfolio narrative that shows both data skills and professional context.

What May Be Driving the Shift

  • Lower average cost compared with traditional burial.
  • Geographic mobility of families and changing memorial preferences.
  • More flexible memorialization options.
  • Changing religious, cultural, and consumer expectations.

These are plausible contributors, not final causal claims. Further research would be needed to measure each factor's impact.

Data Confidence

Source confidence was tracked rather than hidden. COVID-era data from 2021-2022 can vary by source because pandemic conditions affected reporting, mortality, and funeral operations.

Preferred sources for a final public version would include CANA and NFDA, with other sources kept as context or comparison notes.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Data collection and spreadsheet organization.
  • Data cleaning, source tracking, and confidence labeling.
  • Calculated fields, including a cremation-burial preference gap.
  • Tableau visualization and dashboard storytelling.
  • Guided troubleshooting and iteration documented as part of the learning process.
